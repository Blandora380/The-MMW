Android intro implementation

The native renderer in ScorePreview.cpp ports the source AviUtl2 composition
pkg/pjsekaioverlay/main2_16-9_1920x1080.object. A reference copy is included in
source/main2_16-9_1920x1080.object. The quadratic deceleration follows the Lua
formula in dependencies/aviutl2/Script/ANM_ssd/@加減速TRA.tra (param=2), included
as source/easing.tra. AviUtl-specific host APIs are implemented by the native
renderer rather than requiring AviUtl on Android.

Reference timing is 300 frames at 60 fps (5 seconds): the panel settles over
frames 0-111, gradient sweeps run at 60-179 and 180-299, and the foreground
card fades out at 270-299. Gameplay fades in over the next 96 frames. The
4:3 template's 75% scale and bottom alignment are also preserved.

RodinNTLG Pro EB and DB are loaded from res/fonts when available. The fonts
in this personal Android build were copied from the user's installed fonts;
their original font licenses and ownership continue to apply.
